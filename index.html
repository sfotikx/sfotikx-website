About SFOTIKX

Building practical R&D partnerships across the life sciences.

SFOTIKX Ltd is a UK-based pharmaceutical and scientific R&D company supporting research, development and innovation across the life sciences. We bring scientific expertise and a collaborative approach to a range of pharmaceutical and technical challenges, from early-stage research and development through to technology-focused projects.

Our work spans pharmaceutical science, formulation, materials science, analytical development and related areas. We undertake collaborative R&D, outsourced research, scientific and technical consultancy, and technology development in partnership with pharmaceutical, biotechnology and research organisations.

Capabilities

Scientific expertise that can flex around the problem.

Our capabilities span pharmaceutical science, formulation, materials science, analytical development and related R&D, allowing us to support projects from scientific investigation through to technology-focused development.

Pharmaceutical Research & Development

Scientific support across pharmaceutical development, from early-stage research and problem definition through to experimental development and technical evaluation.

Formulation & Preformulation

Preformulation studies, formulation development, physicochemical assessment and investigation of factors influencing pharmaceutical performance and stability.

Solid-State & Materials Science

Polymorph, salt and cocrystal development, crystal engineering, solid-form assessment, phase behaviour and materials-related research.

Crystallisation & Particle Engineering

Crystallisation and precipitation studies, supersaturation, nucleation and crystal growth, particle-property evaluation and process optimisation.

Analytical & Characterisation

Application of complementary analytical and characterisation approaches to understand materials, formulations and pharmaceutical systems and support R&D decisions.

Scientific Consultancy & Collaborative R&D

Outsourced research, technical consultancy and collaborative R&D for pharmaceutical, biotechnology and research organisations, including technology-development projects.
